## FAQ

**Q. Events do not appear in Month or Year View**  
A. In Edit Event, turn on Show in Month and Year Views. Events with a time that you add in Day View start with this off. When it is off, the event appears neither on the wheel nor in Next in Month and Year Views. For Calendar Import, turn off All-Day Events Only in Month and Year Views in Calendar Import in Settings.

**Q. How do I edit an event?**  
A. Tap an event on the wheel, or a row in Next, once to open Edit Event.

**Q. An event cannot be moved**  
A. Touch and hold the event on the wheel, then drag without lifting your finger. After you select it, you can also drag inside the dotted frame to move it.

**Q. How do I switch between year, month and day?**  
A. Tap the switch button at the bottom right. On the widget, each tap anywhere inside the wheel, except the dates around the edge, switches the view.

**Q. How do I add an event with a tag?**  
A. Tap ＋ at the bottom right to open Add Event, then drag and drop a tag onto the wheel. Tap a tag to set the date and time first. Touch and hold a tag to edit it.

**Q. Events do not appear on another device**  
A. Log in with the same account on both devices, from Account in Settings. Colors and display settings are not synced.

**Q. The widget shows a lock**  
A. The widget is available with TimeSpinner Plus.

**Q. How do I change the language?**  
A. Choose 日本語, English, 한국어, 简体中文 or 繁體中文 from Language in Settings. System follows the iPhone language setting.

**Q. How do I delete my account?**  
A. In Settings, open Account and tap Delete Account. This deletes the events, tags and account in the cloud. Events and tags on this iPhone stay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. I want to delete my account.**
A. You can delete it from "Settings" → "Delete Account" inside the app. After deletion, all data is erased.

**Q. I want to leave my team.**
A. You are automatically removed from the team in the following cases:
1. When the weekly goal is not achieved
2. When you sign out
3. When you delete your account

**Q. How do I cancel Premium?**
A. You can manage your Darwin subscription from your iPhone's "Settings" → "Apple ID" → "Subscriptions".

# Privacy Policy

**Jet Log** (iOS / macOS / watchOS, hereinafter "the App") protects users' personal information appropriately in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: June 28, 2026

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information to provide its service.

### 1-1. Information you provide directly
- Email address (only if you sign up with email authentication)
- Tag information (name, color, icon)
- Tracking logs (tag, start time, end time, tracked duration)
- Display settings (accent color, chart type, language, theme, breakdown / average-line visibility)

### 1-2. Information provided by authentication providers
- When using Sign in with Apple: the private relay email address or real email address provided by Apple (depending on your choice)

### 1-3. Information collected automatically
- The user identifier (UID) issued by Firebase Authentication
- Account creation date and time

The App does not collect any browsing history, location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or other information beyond the above.

---

## 2. Purpose of Use

We use the collected information only for the following purposes.

- Authentication and account management for the App
- Storing and displaying the tags, tracking logs, and statistics you record
- Synchronizing data across iOS / macOS / watchOS when signed in with the same account
- Improving the service and considering new features
- Fulfilling legal obligations

The App does not use your data for advertising or marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Provision to Third Parties

Except with your consent or as required by law, we do not provide your personal information to third parties.

However, the following services are essential to providing the App's functionality, and information is processed in accordance with each provider's terms.

| Service | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication | Google LLC | User authentication |
| Cloud Firestore | Google LLC | Storing and syncing app data |
| Sign in with Apple | Apple Inc. | Authentication via Apple ID |

Please refer to each company's official page for their privacy policies.

The App displays no advertising (such as Google AdMob) and provides no information to advertising providers.

---

## 4. Publicly Shared Information

The App has no social features (such as following, likes, or public profiles). All data you record — tags, tracking logs, statistics, and so on — is accessible only by your own account and is never made public to other users.

Your email address and account settings are also never made public to other users.

---

## 5. Data Storage

User data is securely stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Firebase holds SOC 1/2/3 and ISO 27001/27017/27018 certifications and complies with industry-standard security practices. Each device (iOS / macOS / watchOS) keeps a local cache for offline use.

---

## 6. Data Retention Period

Data is retained for as long as you hold an account. If you delete your account, the related personal information and recorded content are, in principle, deleted promptly. However, it may temporarily remain in system backups.

---

## 7. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from "Settings" → "Delete Account" inside the App, and all data associated with you (tags, tracking logs, display settings, etc.) is completely deleted. Please note that deletion cannot be undone.

---

## 8. Data Sync and Cross-Device Sharing

The App is available on iOS / macOS / watchOS, and by signing in with the same account, your tags, tracking logs, and settings are automatically synchronized across devices. Synchronization is performed via Cloud Firestore.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 is using the App, we may delete the account.

---

## 10. International Data Transfers

Data collected by the App may be stored and processed on servers outside Japan through the infrastructure of Google Firebase and Apple. By using the App, you consent to such cross-border data transfers.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised in response to changes in laws or the service. For significant changes, we will notify you within the App or by email.

# Privacy Policy (Android)

**TimeSpinner - Circle Calendar** (Android, hereinafter "the App") protects users' personal information appropriately in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

---

## 1. Information the App Uses

### 1-1. Events and tags
Events and tags you create in the App are stored on your device. If you do not log in, events and tags never leave your device.

### 1-2. Account and sync (optional)
Only if you log in from Account in Settings, the App stores the following in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, located in the Tokyo region) and syncs events and tags across devices logged in to the same account:

- Your email address (when you create an account with an email address; when you log in with Google, the email address of your Google account)
- Your account ID
- The contents of your events and tags

Your password is kept by Firebase, and the developer cannot see it. When you log in with Google, the name and profile photo URL of your Google account are also stored in Firebase Authentication. The App does not show or use them.

Even if you do not log in, the App creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account (a random ID) at launch to count the number of users. The anonymous account is never linked to your events, tags, email address or any other information that identifies you.

### 1-3. Calendar Integration (TimeSpinner Plus)
**Calendar Import**: If you turn on Calendar Import in Settings and allow access to calendars, the App reads events in the calendars on your device (including Google and other accounts added to the device) and shows them on the wheel. Imported events are only shown on your device. They are never stored, synced or sent.

**Write to Calendar**: If you turn on Write to Calendar in Settings and allow access to calendars, the App writes its events (title, date and time, repeat and notes) to the TimeSpinner calendar on your device. Events you edit, add or delete in the TimeSpinner calendar are also changed in the App. The TimeSpinner calendar is created only on your device and is not sent to Google or other accounts or to the developer's servers. When you turn it off, the TimeSpinner calendar is deleted and the events in the App are kept.

### 1-4. Settings stored on your device
Colors and display settings are stored only on your device and are not synced.

### 1-5. Purchases
Purchases of TimeSpinner Plus are processed by Google Play. The App checks your purchase status through Google Play and does not receive your payment information.

### 1-6. Advertising
If you do not have TimeSpinner Plus, the App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location estimated from it, data on ads shown and tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.

The App does not collect your contacts, precise location or browsing history. Your email address is used only if you log in.

---

## 2. Purpose of Use

- Saving the events you create and showing them on the wheel
- Syncing events and tags across devices logged in to the same account
- Counting the number of users
- Unlocking TimeSpinner Plus for people who purchased it
- Showing ads to people who do not have TimeSpinner Plus

---

## 3. Provision to Third Parties

Except as described below or as required by law, we do not provide your information to third parties.

| Service | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Google LLC | Showing ads (only without TimeSpinner Plus) |
| Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore | Google LLC | Account authentication, storing and syncing events and tags, counting users |
| Google Sign-In | Google LLC | Logging in with a Google account |
| Google Play Billing | Google LLC | Purchases of TimeSpinner Plus |

Please refer to each company's official page for their privacy policies.

---

## 4. Data Deletion

You can delete events and tags in the App. If you are logged in, events and tags you delete on your device are also deleted from the cloud.

Tapping Delete Account in Account in Settings deletes the events and tags stored in the cloud and your account, including your email address. Events and tags on your device remain. They also remain on your device when you log out.

If you want to delete your account after deleting the App, contact us at the address below from the email address you used to log in. We will delete your account and the events and tags stored in the cloud.

Deleting the App removes all events and settings stored on your device.

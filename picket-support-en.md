## FAQ

**Q. The widget does not change after I take a photo with the Camera app**  
A. Tap the refresh button on the widget, or set up Automatic Update in Settings.

**Q. Automatic Update is slow**  
A. Automatic Update runs through the Shortcuts app, so it can take some time. To update right away, tap the refresh button on the widget.

**Q. The widget shows no photo**  
A. Check Photos Access in Settings. If it is not allowed, tap Open Settings and allow access.

**Q. The part of the photo I want is cut off**  
A. On the Layout tab, drag the preview to move the photo, and pinch to zoom in or out.

**Q. I want to hide the buttons on the widget**  
A. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and turn off Hide Button or Refresh Button.

**Q. The photo disappeared from the Lock Screen**  
A. iOS ends it after 8 hours. Open Picket to show it again.

**Q. I want to change the language**  
A. In Settings, choose from 日本語, English, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文 under Language. System follows the language of your iPhone.

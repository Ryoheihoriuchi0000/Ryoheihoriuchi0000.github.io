## FAQ

**Q. How do I get started?**  
A. Open "Edit lists" from the button at the top right of the Progress tab, add a book or subject, and set its total (the number of problems, pages, etc.). Tap the ＋ button on the Home Screen widget to add 1 to the completed count, so you can record progress without opening the App.

**Q. How do I see a target pace (days left / per day)?**  
A. On the Goals tab, set a start date and a deadline for each step. The days left, the amount needed per day, and the ideal-pace line on the progress bar appear, so you can tell at a glance whether you are on track.

**Q. The widget doesn't appear / doesn't update**  
A. Touch and hold the Home Screen → "+" → choose Pace to add it. If it looks out of date, open the App once to bring it up to date.

**Q. How do I change the step shown on the widget?**  
A. Touch and hold the widget → "Edit Widget" → choose it in "Step to show". Each widget can show its own step.

**Q. How do I change the progress marker icon or size?**  
A. Go to Settings → Progress marker to change the icon shown at your current position (runner, bike, skateboard, etc.) and its size. The change syncs to all devices on the same account.

**Q. How do I change the language?**  
A. Go to Settings → Language and choose 日本語, English, 한국어, or 繁體中文. "System" follows your device's language.

**Q. How do I move or sync my data to another device?**  
A. Sign in with the same Apple ID or email address, and the data stored on Firebase syncs automatically. You can use the same progress on both iPhone and iPad.

**Q. I signed in with Apple and can't change my email address**  
A. For accounts linked to an Apple ID, the email address is managed by Apple and can't be changed in the App. To change it, go to Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security on your device.

**Q. How do I delete my account?**  
A. Go to Settings → Delete account in the App. All your data, including steps, lists, goals, and settings, is permanently deleted.

# Privacy Policy

**Pace** (iOS, hereinafter "the App") protects users' personal information appropriately in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information to provide its service.

### 1-1. Information you provide
- Email address (only if you sign up with email)
- Progress data (step names and colors, list names, totals and completed counts, goal periods)
- Display settings (progress marker type and size, theme, language)

### 1-2. Information provided by the sign-in provider
- If you use Sign in with Apple: the private relay email address or your real email address provided by Apple (your choice)

### 1-3. Information collected automatically
- The user identifier (UID) issued by Firebase Authentication
- Account creation date and time

### 1-4. Information collected to show ads
If you do not use Pace Plus, Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, collects the following information to show ads.

- Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
- Records of ads shown and ads tapped
- Diagnostic information such as crashes and launch time
- IP address (may be used to estimate your approximate region)

The App itself does not collect any other activity history, location, contacts, or similar information.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information only for the following purposes.

- Sign-in and account management
- Saving and showing the progress you record (steps, lists, goals, settings)
- Syncing data between iPhone and iPad signed in to the same account
- Improving the service and considering new features
- Showing ads (only the information in 1-4)
- Meeting legal obligations

We never use your progress data or email address for advertising or marketing.

---

## 3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share personal information with third parties except with your consent or as required by law.

The following services are essential to the App and process information under their own terms.

| Service | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication | Google LLC | Sign-in |
| Cloud Firestore | Google LLC | Storing and syncing app data |
| Sign in with Apple | Apple Inc. | Sign-in with Apple ID |
| Google AdMob | Google LLC | Showing ads |

Please see each provider's official privacy policy.

### About ads

The App shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen (just above the tab bar), and a full-screen ad every few times you switch the bottom tabs. Ads are delivered by Google LLC (AdMob). No ads are shown on the first launch after installing or updating, or during the tutorial.

When you first launch the App, it asks for permission to track. Only if you allow it is your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) used to tailor ads. All features of the App work even if you do not allow it. You can change this later in the device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you use Pace Plus, no ads are shown and no information is collected for ads.

---

## 4. Public Information

The App has no social features (follows, likes, public profiles, etc.). All data you record, including your progress, can be accessed **only by your own account** and is never shown to other users.

Your email address and account settings are not shown to other users either.

---

## 5. Data Storage

User data is stored securely on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Firebase holds SOC 1/2/3 and ISO 27001/27017/27018 certifications and follows industry-standard security practices. Each device keeps a local cache for offline use and for the widget.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account, related personal information and records are deleted promptly in principle. They may remain temporarily in system backups.

---

## 7. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the App. All data tied to you (steps, lists, goals, settings, etc.) is permanently deleted. It cannot be restored after deletion.

---

## 8. Sync Across Devices

The App is available on iPhone and iPad. When you sign in to the same account, your progress and settings are synced automatically between devices through Cloud Firestore.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 is using the App, we may delete the account.

---

## 10. International Data Transfers

Data collected by the App may be stored and processed on servers outside Japan through the infrastructure of Google Firebase and Apple. By using the App, you agree to these cross-border transfers.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when laws or the service change. We will notify you in the App or by email of any important changes.

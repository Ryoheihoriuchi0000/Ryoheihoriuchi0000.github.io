Five verbs

Every app
fits into one
of five verbs.

Not by jumbled home-screen icons
or vaguely-named folders,
but by what you're about to do when you pick up your phone or laptop.
The hundreds of apps on your device
fit into exactly these five.

Receive Record Communicate Enjoy Settings
Receive

Receive

Take in what the world sends your way. Notifications, messages, schedules, news, timelines. "Read", "see", "find out" — actions that flow inward.

Apps like
Mail Calendar News X (Twitter) RSS reader Weather Podcast inbox
Record

Record

Take what's inside you — or in front of you — and put it somewhere outside. Write, capture, archive. A flow that goes inside → out.

Apps like
Notes Notion Obsidian Bear Photos Voice Memos Budget tracker Journal
Communicate

Communicate

Exchange with another person, back and forth. Receiving and sending keep alternating — different from one-way "Receive", different from inward "Record". The space between people.

Apps like
Messages LINE Slack Discord Phone FaceTime Zoom ChatGPT
Enjoy

Enjoy

Use your device for yourself, not for a task. Listen to music, watch a video, play a game, dive into content. Take the time to consume — knowingly, intentionally.

Apps like
Music Spotify YouTube Netflix Podcasts Streaming radio Games E-books
Settings

Settings

Tune your apps, devices, and surroundings. Change a setting, check a balance, look at a status and make a small adjustment. Not passive, not creative — just keeping things in order.

Apps like
Settings Wi-Fi manager Banking Finance tracker Health Smart home Password manager

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. What we collect

・Your email address (if you register with email) ・The identifier and email address Apple provides (if you sign in with Apple) ・The contents of your cards (text, tables, checklists, handwriting, photos, videos, files, links and recordings) ・Card settings such as folder names, pins and paper colour

2. If you do not sign in

Cards stay on your device only and are never sent to our servers. The widget simply reads the shared storage (App Group) on your device.

3. If you sign in

To sync between devices on the same account, the contents of your cards are stored in Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), provided by Google LLC. Text and tables are stored in Tokyo (asia-northeast1) and attachments such as photos in the western United States (us-west1). We do not read your content.

4. Why we use it

・To sync cards between your devices ・To check your ExtraSticky Plus purchase ・To respond to abuse ・To improve the App

5. Sharing with others

We do not share what we collect with third parties, except where the law requires it.

6. Payment information

Apple handles payment for ExtraSticky Plus. We never receive card numbers or other payment details.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Deleting your account deletes your cards and attachments from the cloud. Cards on your device remain until you delete the App. Attachments no longer used by any card (photos, handwriting, recordings and so on) are removed from the cloud after at least 30 days. The copies on your device remain.

8. Device permissions

・Photos: used only when you pick a photo or video to put on a card ・Camera: used only when you take a shot to put on a card ・Microphone: used only when you add a recording ・Each is used only for the action you asked for, and never otherwise

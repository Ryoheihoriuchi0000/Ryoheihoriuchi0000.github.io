# Privacy Policy

**Picket** (iOS, the "App") handles your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

---

## 1. Information the App Uses

### 1-1. Photos
If you allow Photos access, the App reads up to your four latest photos and saves resized images in the widget's storage on your device. Photos are not sent off your device. The developer cannot see your photos.

### 1-2. Camera
The camera is used to take photos in the App. Photos you take are saved to the Photos app.

### 1-3. Settings Saved on Your Device
Layout, photo position and zoom, appearance and language settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts.

### 1-4. Purchases
Apple processes Picket Plus purchases. The App checks the purchase status through Apple and does not receive your payment information.

### 1-5. Ads
If you do not have Picket Plus, the App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address and the approximate location estimated from it, data on ads shown and tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics to show and measure ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can change the tracking permission at any time in the Settings app.

The App does not collect your name, contact details, precise location or browsing history.

---

## 2. Purposes of Use

- To show your latest photos on the widget and the Lock Screen
- To provide features to users who purchase Picket Plus
- To show ads to users without Picket Plus

---

## 3. Sharing with Third Parties

Except as listed below or as required by law, information is not shared with third parties.

| Service | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Google LLC | Showing ads (only without Picket Plus) |
| App Store / StoreKit | Apple Inc. | Picket Plus purchases |

See each company's official page for its privacy policy.

---

## 4. Deleting Data

Deleting the App deletes all resized images and settings saved on your device. Photos in the Photos app are not deleted.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in response to laws or changes in services. Important changes are announced in the App.
